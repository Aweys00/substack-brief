# Running themes

One section per theme. Each entry is dated and names the post it came from, so the brief can show how a view has changed over time.

## Nvidia leadership
- 2026-11-12, Tae Kim, "Jensen Huang Resigns": Huang resigned as CEO and director; Colette Kress interim. Kim says Nvidia can thrive under Rene Haas or Ian Buck, must retain Jonah Alben, and is undervalued at about $160 (about 10x forward earnings, his figure, unverified). Flags risk of tighter China export rules.
- 2026-11-15, news check: FT/Bloomberg report Huang discussing a comeback and the board approaching Haas. Earnings due 17 Nov.

## AI buildout: demand versus constraints
- 2026-11-09, Gaetano, "The AI Selloff": tech sector fell 8.23% in the week to 6 Nov. Anthropic run rate above $80B (WSJ); Q3 revenue $17B; revenue per compute dollar fell to $1.72 from $2.08 due to about $5B of short-term capacity.
- 2026-11-10, Vik, "Why AI Data Center Constraints Make Efficiency More Valuable": Texas-led permitting opposition after the midterms. Constraint is power or site approval depending on region. Favours compute per MW: 800 VDC (sidecars, then row-level, then solid-state transformers), inference accelerators, optics between racks. Kyber rack reportedly delayed.
- 2026-11-12, Gaetano, "CoreWeave Finally Helped the AI Trade": financing worry eased; prepayments, contract-backed lending, raised capex floor. Verified: Q3 revenue $3.66B, backlog about $132.5B.

## Model pricing and lab economics
- 2026-11-13, Jamin Ball, "The Frontier Blinks": frontier price cut 20% to $8/$40 per million tokens alongside a 30% speed gain. Expects labs to keep passing efficiency gains through.

## Memory
