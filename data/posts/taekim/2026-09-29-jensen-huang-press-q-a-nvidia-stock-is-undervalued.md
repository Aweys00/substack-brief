# Jensen Huang Press Q&A: 'Nvidia Stock Is Undervalued'

On Monday, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang answered questions from reporters at The Korea Society’s annual gala in New York. The executive received this year’s prestigious Van Fleet Award for his contributions to strengthening relations between Korea and the United States.

On stock buybacks:

“We’re really fortunate that NVIDIA is at the center of the largest industrial infrastructure build out in human history. We’re going to generate a lot of cash in the future. Now of course, we’ll continue to make strategic investments and operational investments and we have to secure supply chain commitments and so on and so forth.
