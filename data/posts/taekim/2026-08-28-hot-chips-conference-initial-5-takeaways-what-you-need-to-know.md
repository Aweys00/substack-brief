# Hot Chips Conference Initial 5 Takeaways. What You Need to Know

I LOVED Hot Chips. It has easily become my favorite conference of the year.

Imagine hundreds of the smartest chip engineers in the world listening to roughly 40 technical sessions on the new chip technologies they’ve been working on and their dreams of the future, all shared in an authentic way.

You can tell that even though they compete in the marketplace, they are chip nerds at heart rooting for each other to create the most magical, life-changing products possible. I was inspired and in awe of this special community. I look forward to coming back again and again in the years to come. I can’t recommend it enough.

What did I learn? A lot. I went to nearly every session. They were incredibly technically dense. I’m going to take my time going over the hundreds of slides and the speaker recordings again once I fly back home.

But the real value was in the side conversations in the hallways, at breakfast, lunch, breaks, and receptions. Everyone was so friendly and open. You can stand in line for lunch and meet the lead engineer of a major historical processor. These engineers are the ones who know what is really happening inside the industry. I met and talked to dozens of them.
