# OCP Day One: AMD CEO Sees Strong Demand for AI Inference and CPUs

AMD CEO Lisa Su is emphatic and optimistic about the current demand for AI.

“We’re in a world where computing equates to intelligence, and if you have a chance to be more intelligent, why wouldn’t you try to get more capability?” she said Monday during her OCP Global Summit keynote in San Jose. “Every time I look at these [demand] charts, they tend to keep going up and to the right.”
