# New here? Start with the article map

I built a site for my Substack subscribers.

[Go Damnang](https://map.damnang.com)

Damnang Research now holds over a hundred essays and more than 400 reader questions. The archive keeps growing, which makes it harder to know where to start.

If you subscribed recently, the map is the fastest way in.

##### How to use it

Open the "Start here" tab and pick how you read: as an investor or as an engineer.

You get three essays to start with. Investors begin with "Why HBM Is So Hard to Make" and "The Bottleneck Map." Engineers get the HBM essay plus "CPO, Fully Dissected."

Then use the search box or the Threads and Essays filters to find topics: CPO, glass substrates, EUV lithography, HBM, power and thermals. Series have a "view all parts" link so you can read them in sequence.

Just click the “Free only” tab to browse all the free articles. Around 30% of my articles are currently free to read, and I plan to keep it that way.

The "Ask Damnang" tab holds reader questions. There are 413, with 225 answered. Search the archive there. Full answers live on Substack, and each question links back here.

Interested in collaborating with me? Click the “Work with Damnang” button or visit research.damnang.com and tell me what you have in mind.
