# TEF and OCP Global Summit 2026: Game Plan

OCP Global Summit (Oct 12-15) is highly anticipated this year, but I also signed up for a 2-day session (Oct 7-8) held by the Ethernet Alliance called Technology Exploration Forum (TEF) on “The AI Future: Ethernet.” Semicon West overlaps with OCP Global Summit this year sadly, so I might have to skip.

Subscribe to have these newsletters in your inbox. Upgrade for the whole enchilada.

#### TEF 2026

TEF is two-days of panel events entirely around networking, and Ethernet is getting considerably important in the AI age. I like the idea of industry experts sitting down to talk about stuff instead of presentation talks because the unscripted nature of panels brings out a lot more insights.

[Day 1](https://ethernetalliance.org/tef-2026-day-1-agenda/) consists of the following topics, with panelists from the listed companies:

-

400 Gbps signaling over copper: TE Connectivity, Huawei, and Broadcom.

-

Optical infrastructure challenges: Commscope, Panduit, Corning.

-

State of multicore fiber: Panduit, Corning, Terahop, CommScope

There are also presentations from Oracle, Corning, and Meta.

[Day 2](https://ethernetalliance.org/tef-2026-day-2-agenda/)is more panel heavy than the first:

-

IMDD optics: Huawei, Coherent, Cisco, Ranovus

-

Coherent ethernet: Huawei, Cisco, Ciena, Nokia

-

Optical packaging: Huawei, Arista, Molex, Eliyan

-

Testing for AI networks: Wilder Tech, Viavi, Keysight

This event was pricey, and I wonder how much overlap there will be with OCP content. You will notice that the amount of optics content outweighs copper in the discussion topics. The future of AI networking is clearly going to light, except for some specific use cases where copper will continue to live.

#### OCP Global Summit

This conference is sold out, as was Hot Chips before the event. OCP has a high number of parallel events and its impossible to cover everything without an army of attendees. Look at the [full schedule](https://2026ocpglobal.fnvirtual.app/a/schedule/).

I’m going to choose two main focus areas: Networking and Power. What am I chalking out below is mostly me thinking aloud of how I’m going to approach this event. If you’re attending, hopefully this gives you a broad map of how to go about it. Usually I end up attending only 60-70% of what I plan to because I end up talking to a lot of people and also get info overloaded. I also have meetings set up with several companies too which I have to juggle (Eliyan, Credo, etc.)

##### Day 1 (Monday): Executive Sessions

Day 1 is entirely executive sessions from a wide variety of speakers, but the program is thankfully linear. I expect mostly high level talks, with each company trying to showcase their products. Good for high level stuff and (human) networking.

##### Day 2 (Tuesday): Keynotes + Sessions

Day 2 is full of keynotes before lunch, which will be another broad “sit-and-listen” session. Nothing to expect but pretty looking slides with few surprises. The afternoon session will require a balance between attending networking sessions and expo halls sessions. I’ve listed only about 5 talks max I would like to hit. I will spend rest of the time talking / walking show floor.

Networking Sessions:

-

Meta — Tue, October 13, 2:30pm - 2:45pm | SJCC - Concourse Level - 210AE: Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale: From Link Management to Production Reliability

-

Why: I want to understand the difficulty in deploying LPO at high speeds, and what that means for DSPs.

Expo Hall Sessions:

-

DELTA — Tue, October 13, 12:50pm - 1:05pm | SJCC - Concourse Level - Expo Hall Theater: Microgrids at Scale: Resilient Power Infrastructure for AI Data Centers presented by Delta Electronics

-

Why: How power is deployed drives how much downstream compute, networking, and storage will be needed.

-

Credo(?) — Tue, October 13, 3:30pm - 3:45pm | SJCC - Concourse Level - Expo Hall Theater: Going Wide: Scalable and Reliable Optical Interconnects for AI Clusters

-

Why: I want to see what hardware they have, and how well it works. I am also meeting with them 1:1 earlier that day.

Power Sessions (overlap, so in order of priority):

-

NVIDIA — Tue, October 13, 4:10pm - 4:30pm | SJCC - Concourse Level - 210CG: 800 VDC AI Factories: From Architecture Alignment to Industry Deployment

-

Why: Want to understand where we are in 800VDC deployments, and what the challenges are.

-

NVIDIA — Tue, October 13, 4:25pm - 4:40pm | SJCC - Lower Level - LL21DE: Intelligent Power Smoothing at the Vera-Rubin Rack

-

Why: At Hot Chips, NVIDIA claimed some TCO advantages when they could smooth out power spikes.

##### Day 3 (Wednesday): Mission Impossible

The AI Open DC: Power Distribution and OCP Special Focus: Photonics sessions run in parallel all day. It’s impossible to do both with one meat jacket. The only saving grace is that both sessions are the same level, so easy to keep swapping to some extent. All talks on both tracks seem interesting, and would consider this the meat and potatoes day of the conference. Too many fun ones to break down individually. For reference:

-

Power = Concourse Level, 210CG

-

Photonics = Concourse Level, 210AE
