# The AI Networking Trade Is Being Repriced

There is pain in the AI trade right now.

Networking is one area that has been hit especially hard due to it’s elevated multiples & reliance on future growth.

As the market is questioning the durability of AI capex and ROI these companies are experiencing a complete repricing.

There is strong visibility in the demand that these companies have and they should all continue to execute extremely well over the coming months. But at the side time the market can (and it obviously has been) rewarding that execution with a lower multiple.

My goal right now is to very slowly add into these positions at depressed prices, while also trying to diversify and find opportunities that are not exposed to the AI capex cycle that I want to own regardless of what happens to the AI trade next.

My goal is to pull on different levers as I see fit with what the market is presenting.

So on my Substack here I want to continue to cover the optical, physical AI and hyperscaler stocks, while also presenting new tickers that I am interested in that I will position in to diversify.
