# Is This a Major Beneficiary of 24/5 Trading?

US stocks are about to become a lot easier to trade while most of us are asleep.

We talked briefly about 24/5 trading recently.

I wanted to spend more time on one company that could benefit, and the more I looked through the brokers’ calls, the more interesting this got.

They’re opening up access to US stocks around the world.

At the same time, customers are starting to let AI agents place trades for them.

I think both could bring more activity into the market. And there’s a company already sitting in the middle of a lot of those orders.

Nasdaq is targeting December 6 for its expanded trading schedule.

That would give someone in Korea a more established exchange on which to trade US stocks during their own day.

Brokers already offer overnight trading through other venues, so there’s actual activity to build on.

The SEC measured $7.4 billion in average daily overnight stock trading in August. Still only 0.8% of US equity dollar volume.

Then on top of this I looked at what’s happening with agents, as I was curious if this could potentially be another growth engine for this company. Robinhood reported almost 200,000 agentic accounts and more than $1.6 billion of cumulative trading volume through them.

Interactive Brokers and many others will be opening up autonomous trading too.

There’s money moving through these products already, even if it’s early.

Below I’m going through how this company makes money, which brokers already send it orders and how agents could add to its growth. Then we’ll get into the earnings and what the stock could be worth.
